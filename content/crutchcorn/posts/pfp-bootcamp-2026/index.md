Our first bootcamp was a "full-circle" moment for Playful Programming. [In 2018, I taught at a software education bootcamp](/posts/a-complete-history-of-uu/). While I enjoyed teaching, I found the practices of this bootcamp to be predatory against the students and, as such, I quit. Per the suggestion of the students I taught there, I started Playful Programming to continue teaching software engineering.

It's been many years since then and in that time we've grown into [a proper non-profit](/posts/playful-programming-non-profit/). Last year, [one of our board members, Ade](/people/whatade/), suggested that we take our educational resources "offline" and into the real world.

So, backed by an incredible team, we did just that!


# Who was involved

Anyone that's tried to host an educational event can tell you: There's a lot that goes into planning.

Luckily for us, we had an incredible team behind our efforts. We had two sets of attendees:

1) A virtual group hosted in our Discord server
2) An in-person group hosted in Sacramento, California
