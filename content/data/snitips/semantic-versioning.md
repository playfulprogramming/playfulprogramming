Semantic Versioning numbers releases as `MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH` around a declared public API. After `1.0.0`, major marks incompatible changes, minor adds backward-compatible functionality, and patch contains backward-compatible fixes. Versions before `1.0.0` indicate initial development and may change incompatibly.
